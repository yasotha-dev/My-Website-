About InAmigos

InAmigos Foundation is a student-driven youth organization dedicated to creating a positive impact in society. We focus on community development, education, environmental sustainability, and social awareness campaigns.

Through collaborative efforts, internships, and volunteer drives, our team works endlessly to uplift underprivileged sections of society and foster leadership among young changemakers.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Shiksha

Providing basic education material, books, and mentorship to underprivileged children to bridge the educational gap.

Project Harit

Environmental cleanliness and tree plantation drives across cities to promote sustainability and green living.

Project Annapurna

Food distribution campaigns aimed at ensuring no one goes to sleep hungry in our target local communities.
